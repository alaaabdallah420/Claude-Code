## Decided
- **Next video title:** "Why Even Orcas Are Terrified of Pilot Whales"
  - Based on the formula "Why The Entire Ocean Is Terrified of [Animal]" (inspiration: https://youtu.be/ACQZtdWXpcc).
  - Factual basis: long-finned pilot whales have been recorded chasing orcas away in Norway, Iceland, Spain and Australia. Orcas flee from pilot whale calls alone, and nobody knows exactly why (theories: mob size, loud group calls, anti-predator behavior). Sources: sciencealert.com ("one creature of the deep that can scare even orcas away"), whalescientists.com ("killer whales flee at the sound of pilot whales"), hakaimagazine.com ("Iceland's confusing inter-cetacean conflict").
  - Backup titles: "Why Killer Whales Run From Pilot Whales" / "Why The Ocean's Top Predator Is Terrified of Pilot Whales" / "Nothing About Pilot Whales Is Normal... Here's Why"
