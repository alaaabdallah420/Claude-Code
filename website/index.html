FAQ

Before we begin

How long does a documentary take?

Usually 7–10 days for a 15–20 minute film, depending on the amount of 3D and map work. Studio partners get priority scheduling. Rush delivery is available on request.

Are revisions included?

Revisions are billed per round at a fixed rate stated in your proposal. Most projects don’t need them, because the script, style and references are agreed before production starts.

Do you edit Shorts?

Yes, for existing clients and partnerships. Shorts are quoted per batch, usually cut from your long-form films.

What do I need to send?

Your script or voiceover, plus any footage or references. If you don’t have footage, I can source licensed stock and archive footage for you.

Can you match my channel’s style?

Yes. Send two or three videos you admire and I’ll match the pacing, typography, color and sound.

How does payment work?

A 50% deposit reserves your slot and the balance is due on delivery. Partnerships are billed monthly. Invoices come from Alaa Business LLC (USA). PayPal, Wise, Payoneer or bank transfer.
