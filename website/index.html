FAQ

Before we begin

How long does a project take?

Ad packs take 3–5 days. A VSL or a 15–20 minute video usually takes 7–10 days, depending on the motion and 3D work. Monthly clients get priority scheduling, and 48-hour rush delivery is available.

Are revisions included?

Yes, one revision round is included in every project, free of charge. Any extra rounds are $60 each (see Add-ons). Most projects need very few changes, because the script, style and references are agreed before production starts.

Do you edit Shorts?

Yes. Shorts come in packs of 6, 12 or 24, either cut from your long-form videos or edited from your own vertical footage.

What do I need to send?

Your script or voiceover, plus any footage or references. If you don’t have footage, I can source licensed stock and archive footage for you.

Can you match my channel’s style?

Yes. Send two or three videos you admire and I’ll match the pacing, typography, color and sound.

How does payment work?

A 50% deposit reserves your slot and the balance is due on delivery. Partnerships are billed monthly. Invoices are issued by Alaa Business LLC, registered in the USA.
