FAQ

Before we begin

How fast is delivery?

Shorts in 2 days. Documentaries usually take 5–8 days depending on length, 3D scenes and maps. Monthly partners get priority, and 24-hour express is available.

How many revisions do I get?

Two full revision rounds are included in every project. Small fixes after that are free.

What do I need to send?

Your script or voiceover, plus any footage or references. If you don’t have footage, I can source licensed stock and archive footage for you.

Can you match my channel’s style?

Yes. Send 2–3 videos you love and I’ll match the pacing, typography, color and sound.

How does payment work?

50% upfront and 50% on delivery for single projects. Monthly partners are billed monthly. Invoices come from Alaa Business LLC (USA). PayPal, Wise, Payoneer or bank transfer.
