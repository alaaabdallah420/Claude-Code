FAQ

Good questions.

How fast is delivery?

Shorts in 48 hours. Long-form videos usually take 4–7 days depending on length, maps and motion graphics. Retainer clients get priority.

How many revisions do I get?

Two full revision rounds are included in every project. Small fixes after that are free.

How do I send footage?

Google Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer or Frame.io. Whatever is easiest for you.

Can you match my existing style?

Yes. Send 2–3 videos you love (yours or someone else’s) and I’ll match the pacing, typography and sound.

How does payment work?

50% upfront, 50% on delivery for single projects. Retainers are billed monthly. Invoices come from Alaa Business LLC (USA). Payment by PayPal, Wise, Payoneer or bank transfer.
