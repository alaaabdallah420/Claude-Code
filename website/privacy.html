Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Alaa Media is a post-production studio operated by Alaa Business LLC, registered in Wyoming, USA (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information we collect when you visit this website or work with us, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Information you send us. When you contact us by email, WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram or LinkedIn, we receive your name, contact details and anything you include in your message.
  • Project materials. When you become a client, we receive the footage, scripts, audio, brand assets and other files you share for your project.
  • Billing details. To invoice you, we keep your name, company, billing address and payment records.
  • Basic technical data. Our hosting provider may record standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages visited) to keep the site secure and working.

This website has no contact forms, no user accounts and does not set its own advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare proposals.
  • To produce, deliver and support your project.
  • To send invoices and keep financial records required by law.
  • To keep this website secure and improve how it works.

We never sell your personal information.

Third-party services

Some parts of this site are provided by other companies, which may receive technical data such as your IP address when they load:

  • YouTube and Google Drive, when you play a video. Videos load only after you click play.
  • Google Fonts, for the typefaces on this site.
  • cdnjs and unpkg, which deliver the scripts that power scrolling and animation.
  • Our hosting provider, which serves the website.

We also use trusted tools to run the business, such as email, file transfer, messaging and banking services. They process data only as needed to provide their service.

Confidentiality of client work

Your footage, scripts and unreleased content are treated as confidential. We only share them with people who help produce your project, and only when needed. We may show finished, publicly released work in our portfolio unless you ask us not to or we have signed a non-disclosure agreement.

How long we keep data

  • Messages and proposals: as long as needed to handle your enquiry or project.
  • Raw footage and project files: usually up to 90 days after final delivery, then deleted, unless we agree otherwise.
  • Invoices and payment records: as long as tax and accounting laws require.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, except where we must keep it for legal or accounting reasons. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA. To make a request, email us and we will reply within 30 days.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information and project files, including access controls and secure storage. No method of transfer or storage is completely secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
