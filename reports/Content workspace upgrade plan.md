# Swap the GPU first, then add an M4 Mac

The best value upgrade is a **16 GB NVIDIA card**. Buy a used **RTX 4070 Ti Super at about 200-230k DA** if one passes the checks below. If none does, buy a **new RTX 5070 Ti at 294.5k DA** (in stock at Campus Informatique). Add a **~25k DA 850 W Gold ATX 3.1 PSU** and leave the CPU, motherboard and RAM alone. That pairing makes Blender Cycles **+157% to +175% faster** than the current RTX 3060 Ti. It also doubles the 8 GB VRAM ceiling, which is what breaks heavy underwater scenes. Selling the 3060 Ti for about 60-70k DA cuts the net GPU cost to roughly 135-230k DA. For the portable, the value floor is an **M4 Pro MacBook Pro with 24 GB or more**. It roughly matches today's desktop in Cycles (about 99-113% of a 3060 Ti), and Apple laptops lead in After Effects. Every M1 and M2 Mac, even the M2 Max, renders Cycles slower than the machine you already own. The cheapest Ouedkniss MacBooks are often **MDM-locked ex-corporate units at 35-40% off**, and these are not bargains. Buying in France rarely saves money at the parallel rate of about 276 DA/EUR. The one exception is a used RTX 5070 Ti, at roughly 10-15% below Algerian new prices. In practice: about 250k DA buys the desktop upgrade alone, about 500k adds an entry M4 Pro Mac, and about 800k adds a new 5070 Ti, a 4K monitor and extra storage. Above about 1.15M DA you can step up to an RTX 5080 and an M4 Max that works as a second render node.

The used **RTX 3080 and 3080 Ti** are the cheapest speed per dinar. They are the wrong buy anyway, because they keep the scene-size problem at 10-12 GB. They are also old Ampere cards with mining-era wear, and they lack the 50-series video engines. The Blender manual says Cycles spills into system RAM when GPU memory fills. The render usually still finishes, at a performance cost, and fails with an out-of-memory error once host memory runs out too ([Blender Manual](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/render/cycles/gpu_rendering.html)). A render-farm vendor's 2026 guide puts production scenes with 4K textures at **12-16 GB**, and heavy displacement, volumetric and particle scenes at **24 GB or more** ([iRender](https://irendering.net/how-much-vram-do-you-need-for-3d-rendering-a-practical-guide-2026/)). That is exactly the "Into the Blue" mix of ocean displacement, volumetric water and scattered life. **16 GB is the right target.** 24 GB is insurance for only the heaviest simulations.

On that basis the used **RTX 4070 Ti Super** is the value pick. A "10/10" unit at 200k DA costs about the same as a new RTX 5070, yet adds 4 GB of VRAM and about 15% more Cycles speed. The new **RTX 5070 Ti** costs roughly 65-95k DA more for about 7% more Cycles speed, a shop warranty and GDDR7. It also brings **two ninth-generation NVENC encoders** with AV1 support ([NVIDIA](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce/graphics-cards/50-series/rtx-5070-family/)). It is the only option here with hardware decode of 10-bit 4:2:2 H.264/HEVC camera footage, which Puget verified only on the 50 series ([Puget Systems](https://www.pugetsystems.com/labs/articles/verifying-nvidia-geforce-rtx-50-series-performance/)). For an animation-led channel with little camera footage, that decode advantage matters less than the warranty. Choose the 5070 Ti if no used 4070 Ti Super passes the test protocol.

The **used RTX 3090** is a VRAM purchase, not a speed purchase. It renders at roughly RTX 4070 speed and is about 40% slower than a 5070 Ti. It draws 350 W ([NVIDIA](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce/graphics-cards/30-series/rtx-3090-3090ti/)) and needs back-side memory checks, and French used prices are no lower ([leboncoin 3090](https://www.leboncoin.fr/ck/accessoires_informatique/rtx-3090)). Buy one only if a real project file shows peak memory above 16 GB. Avoid AMD for this workflow: the RX 9070 XT is only 19-35% faster than a 3060 Ti in Cycles on HIP. Two price traps also apply. **Click Informatique adds 7-10% to a card sold without a full PC build** ([Click](https://click-dz.com/rtx-5070ti-16gb-zotac-gaming-solid-sff-fan-x3-256bit-gddr7-7-pour-ceux-qui-veulent-lacheter-seule-copie/)). Campus prices flagged out-of-stock (229k and 259.5k for 5070 Ti models) are historical prices, not offers.

The upgrade does not speed up the whole workflow evenly. Puget found After Effects 2D and tracking scores **"essentially identical"** across an RTX 5080, 4080 Super and 3080 Ti, because that work is CPU-bound. AE's 3D score and GPU-heavy Resolve work do scale with the GPU: the 5080 led the 3080 Ti by 44% in AE 3D and by 86% in Resolve LongGOP 4:2:2 ([Puget RTX 5080 review](https://www.pugetsystems.com/labs/articles/nvidia-geforce-rtx-5080-content-creation-review)). Expect **2.5-2.8x faster Cycles renders**, faster Element 3D and Resolve effects, and little change in 2.5D shape-layer comps.

## The rest of the desktop needs a 25k DA PSU and perhaps one drive

The unknown PSU is the one non-GPU item you have to deal with. NVIDIA lists **750 W required system power** for the 3090, 5070 Ti and 4080 Super ([NVIDIA 5070 family](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce/graphics-cards/50-series/rtx-5070-family/); [NVIDIA 4080 family](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce/graphics-cards/40-series/rtx-4080-family/)). MSI recommends **850 W** for i7-class systems with a 4070 Ti Super or 4080 Super ([MSI PSU table](https://www.msi.com/blog/recommended-psu-table)). Open the side panel and read the label. Unless it is a known-good 850 W Gold unit, buy the **MSI MPG A850GS at 24,900 DA** (Campus, in stock), which has a native 12V-2x6 cable ([Campus](https://campusinformatique.com/shop/alimentation-msi-mpg-a850gs-pcie5-850w-80plus-gold/)). The ASUS TUF 850 W runs 26.9-37k DA ([NextGen](https://nextgendz.com/product-detail.php?id=1041)). For an RTX 5080, the **MSI MAG A1000GL at 35,800 DA** gives headroom ([Campus](https://campusinformatique.com/shop/alimentation-msi-mag-a1000gl-1000w-80plus-gold-modulaire-pcie5-0/)). Connector melting reports cluster on 575 W RTX 5090s and some 5080s ([heise](https://heise.de/-10276861); [HotHardware](https://hothardware.com/news/rtx5080-melting-connector)). With a 285-300 W card the risk is lower, but still use the PSU maker's native cable and seat it fully.

**Do not buy RAM.** DDR5 is extremely expensive in Algeria right now: a 2x32 GB DDR5-6000 CL30 kit costs **210-229k DA**, about as much as a used RTX 3090, and no 2x48 GB kit is listed anywhere ([Geekzone](https://www.geekzonedz.com/barrettes-memoire/3239-kingston-fury-beast-rgb-64-go-2-x-32-go-ddr5-6000-mhz-cl30.html); [Campus](https://campusinformatique.com/shop/ram-xpg-64go-2x32go-6000mhz-lancer-blade-cl30-ddr5/)). The existing 64 GB also cushions Cycles when a scene overflows VRAM ([Blender Manual](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/render/cycles/gpu_rendering.html)). Run a memory stability test on the mixed kit. If it is unstable at its rated speed, lower the speed rather than replacing it.

Storage is a cheap quality-of-life gain. A dedicated 2 TB NVMe for projects and AE and Resolve caches costs **45,900 DA (Lexar Thor Pro)** or **65,900 DA (Samsung 990 Pro)** ([NextGen Lexar](https://nextgendz.com/product-detail.php?id=580); [NextGen 990 Pro](https://nextgendz.com/product-detail.php?id=527)). A 4 TB 990 Pro is 118,900 DA ([NextGen](https://nextgendz.com/product-detail.php?id=813)). The 84,900 DA Campus 4 TB price is out of stock and probably stale. For grading and checking 4K uploads, the **ASUS ProArt PA279CV 27-inch 4K at 116,900 DA** is in stock at Lahlou ([Lahlou](https://lahlou-industrie.com/products/ecran-asus-27-proart-pa279cv-4k-uhd-2160p-ips)). The 1440p PA278CV at about 68k DA is the budget option ([Geekzone](https://www.geekzonedz.com/moniteurs/3020-asus-27-led-proart-pa278cv.html)). The Dell U2723QE and LG 27UP850 have no Algerian listings.

Blender is only one axis, and it is the one where the Mac matters least. In Puget's 2024 tests, an **M3 Max MacBook Pro was the fastest system in After Effects, about 15% ahead** of i9-14900K and Ryzen 7950X desktops with RTX 4080 Supers. Macs were also much faster on ProRes and 8-bit H.264/HEVC in Premiere. Desktop RTX PCs were **50-120% faster in DaVinci Resolve** ([Puget Systems](https://www.pugetsystems.com/labs/articles/mac-vs-pc-for-content-creation-2024/)). After Effects 26.2 then gave Macs about a **3.8x uplift in AE's 3D score** ([Puget blog](https://www.pugetsystems.com/blog/2026/04/16/after-effects-update-26-2-improved-puget-bench-3d-results/)). A secondary Mac should therefore be an AE-and-edit machine that can also do Blender lookdev and overflow renders. Two consequences follow. First, **unified memory counts double**: 18 GB on the M3 Pro is tight with Blender, AE and Premiere open together, and 24 GB is the practical minimum. Second, the M1 Max 64 GB at 418-455k DA is a strong AE box but poor value against an M4 Pro. Its large RAM still renders Cycles at less than half the speed of the desktop you are replacing.

The recommended order is a **clean M4 Pro 14-inch with 24 GB or more** (48 GB preferred) as the value pick. Pro-tier chips run cooler, so they suit the 14-inch chassis. Next is an **M4 Max 32-core 36 GB**, which turns the laptop into a second render node at about 3090 class, or an M3 Max as a cheaper Max. If you will render long sequences on the Mac, choose the 16-inch: Notebookcheck measured the 14-inch M5 Max capped at 97 W and losing 7-25% under sustained GPU load ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Apple-s-M5-Max-in-the-MacBook-Pro-16-is-around-15-faster-compared-to-the-MacBook-Pro-14.1250872.0.html)). That test was on the M5 Max; the same behaviour on M3 Max and M4 Max is inferred, not measured. Two compatibility notes apply on macOS. OptiX denoising is unavailable, but OpenImageDenoise is GPU-accelerated on Apple Silicon since Blender 4.1 ([Blender 4.1 notes](https://wiki.blender.org/release_notes/4.1/cycles/)). Video Copilot's Element 3D has a dedicated crash article on Adobe's Apple Silicon page, so test it before relying on it ([Adobe](https://helpx.adobe.com/after-effects/desktop/get-started/technical-requirements/after-effects-apple-silicon.html)).

## France saves money only on a 5070 Ti; bring one item per trip

At the parallel rate of **276 DA/EUR** (Square Port-Saïd, 11 Oct 2026; official rate about 150.92), French used prices mostly match or exceed Ouedkniss ([ObservAlgérie](https://observalgerie.com/2026/10/11/economie/algerie-leuro-marque-une-pause-sur-le-marche-noir-a-combien-sechange-t-il)). On leboncoin, used RTX 3090s at 650-1,050 EUR come to **179-290k DA**, against 190-218k locally. RTX 4070 Ti Supers typically ask 630-800 EUR, or 174-221k DA. The exception is the **RTX 5070 Ti: 950-1,050 EUR used, about 262-290k DA**, roughly 10-15% below Algerian new prices but without a local warranty ([leboncoin 5070 Ti ad](https://www.leboncoin.fr/ad/accessoires_informatique/3281110598); [leboncoin 3090](https://www.leboncoin.fr/ck/accessoires_informatique/rtx-3090)). For Macs, France sells reduced risk rather than a lower price. A Back Market FR 16-inch M1 Max 32 GB at about 473k DA costs more than a clean 64 GB unit on Ouedkniss. Apple Certified Refurbished France currently starts at about 952k DA, for an M5 Pro ([Apple FR](https://www.apple.com/fr/shop/refurbished/mac/macbook-pro)). Algeria has no Apple Store, and no source confirms AppleCare is sold locally, so warranty repairs on a French unit would happen in France ([Apple Community](https://communities.apple.com/fr/thread/253458853)).

Algerian customs (DGD) allow personal goods duty-free up to **150,000 DA**. Some official pages still say 50,000 DA. Above the allowance a **30% flat tax** applies to the excess, but **"téléphones portables et matériels informatiques destinés à l'usage personnel ou familial"** are listed as exempt from that flat-rate tax. Goods that look commercial by nature or quantity lose the treatment ([DGD article 553](https://www.douane.gov.dz/spip.php?article553=&lang=fr); [Algérie360](https://www.algerie360.com/douane-algerie-voyageurs-devises-bijoux-franchise/); [Ulysse 2026](https://ulysse.com/news/douane-algerie-2026-guide-diaspora-devises-vehicules-bagages)). The DGD page returned HTTP 503 during research, so this rests on summaries of it. No GPU-specific rule exists; the only evidence is an anecdotal 2009 forum post ([forumdz](https://www.forumdz.com/topic/14200-informatique-perso-%C3%A0-la-douane-alg%C3%A9rienne/)). It is also unknown which exchange rate customs uses for valuation. If the exemption were refused, a 1,000 EUR GPU would owe almost nothing valued at the official rate, but about 37.8k DA at the parallel rate. A 2,000 EUR laptop would owe about 45.6k or 120.6k DA respectively. These figures are an inference, not a ruling. Carry one item per traveller, unboxed and visibly in use, with the invoice. Since 1 January 2026, travellers must declare cash above 1,000 EUR on entry and exit ([Algérie360](https://www.algerie360.com/douane-algerie-voyageurs-devises-bijoux-franchise/)).

## Test before paying: checklist for used GPUs and Macs

The RTX 3090 carries GDDR6X on both sides of the board, and the back modules are cooled only by the backplate. Owners report **memory-junction temperatures of 100-110 °C** under memory-heavy loads, and drops to about 88 °C after re-padding ([EVGA forum](https://forums.evga.com/3090-Kingpin-others-Get-that-backside-VRAM-cool-m3219773.aspx); [Solent Renegades](https://solent-renegades.co.uk/threads/rtx-3090-high-gddr6x-ram-temperatures-of-110deg-c.42358/)). Sources disagree on the limit, 95 °C versus 105 °C ([HWCooling](https://www.hwcooling.net/doplnene-zahrievanie-vram-a-dalsie-zmeny-v-testoch-grafik/)). Bring a USB stick with GPU-Z, HWiNFO (6.42 or later reads Ampere memory-junction temperature), the Blender Benchmark and OCCT.

| GPU check | Pass | Walk away or renegotiate |
|---|---|---|
| GPU-Z identity and BIOS vs TechPowerUp database | Model, memory size and BIOS match | Mismatched or modified (mining) BIOS |
| Blender Benchmark 4.5 score vs median | Within ~10% (3090 ≈ 5,400; 4070 Ti Super ≈ 7,100; 5070 Ti ≈ 7,600) | More than 10% low suggests throttling or wear |
| 20-30 min OCCT VRAM test + 3D load, logging HWiNFO | Zero errors | Any VRAM error |
| Memory junction temp (GDDR6X cards) | Stays below ~100 °C | Persistent >100 °C means pads need replacing; price it in |
| Hotspot minus core temperature | Under ~20 °C | Larger gap means paste or mount problems |
| Physical inspection | Straight PCB, quiet fans, intact 12V-2x6 or 8-pin plugs | Yellowed or bent PCB, fan wobble, scorched connector |
| Paperwork | Invoice and transferable warranty | No proof of origin |

The test protocol is general practice built from the sources above, not a single published procedure ([HWiNFO forum](https://www.hwinfo.com/forum/threads/rtx-3080-3090-memory-vrm-temperature-sensor.6830/page-3)). For Macs, the decisive risk is organisational lock. Only the owning organisation's Apple Business Manager or School Manager administrator can release a managed device. Organisation-linked Activation Lock can be switched on remotely, and an ABM-assigned Mac re-enrols itself at setup after a reset ([Apple Platform Deployment](https://support.apple.com/guide/deployment/depf4ab94ef1/web); [Jamf Community](https://community.jamf.com/general-discussions-2/new-macbook-neo-unexpectedly-enrolled-in-jamf-mdm-looking-for-an-apple-jamf-admin-s-advice-58981)). Ouedkniss openly lists "MDM bypassed" units ([Ouedkniss](https://www.ouedkniss.com/macbooks-macbook-pro-m1-gris-sideral-cpu-8c-8gb-ram-512gb-ssd-comptage-cycles-127-mdm-bypassed-mostaganem-algeria-d53672018)). Treat "bypassed" as a disclosure that the Mac still belongs to someone else.

| Mac check | How | Walk away if |
|---|---|---|

Re-check every Ouedkniss figure in a real browser before negotiating. None of them could be read from live listing pages; all are undated asking prices from search snapshots. The M4 Pro, M4 Max and M3 Max prices in the Mac table are unverified leads.

## Conclusion

In Algeria today the GPU is the cheap part of a creator workstation and memory is the expensive part. That reverses the usual order. Spend on a 16 GB card, keep the 64 GB you already own, and avoid any plan that needs new DDR5. The same logic, "buy the capability your scenes actually hit", decides the Mac. Its value lies in AE and editing away from the desk, where an M4 Pro already beats the desktop. An M1 Max offers a large RAM figure on the spec sheet but is slow in Blender. The market's biggest discounts, MDM-locked Macs and ex-mining Ampere cards, are discounts for risk you would carry yourself.

Sequence the purchases to limit regret. Upgrade the desktop GPU and PSU first, since that gives the largest speedup per dinar. Then run a real project to read Cycles' peak memory. That number tells you whether 16 GB is enough, or whether a used 3090, or a Max-chip Mac with 36 GB or more of unified memory, would be worth it. Buy the Mac last, once a clean M4 Pro or M4 Max listing is verified, rather than paying full price for an older chip.
